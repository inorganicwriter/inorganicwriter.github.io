### Sirun Li

Undergraduate student at School of Earth and Space Science, Peking University, majoring in Geographic Information Science (GIS), with a double degree in Economics from National School of Development.

Active member of LCPU (Linux Club of Peking University), PKU GeekLab, PILA (PKU Internet Literature Association), and member of Peking iGEM 2024 team. Passionate about GeoAI, open source and interdisciplinary innovation.

Interests: Cooking, Roguelike games, modern music, mechanical models.
