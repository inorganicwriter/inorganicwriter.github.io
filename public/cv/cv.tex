% ========== Selected Research Projects ==========
\section{\textbf{Selected Research Projects}}
\vspace{-0.4mm}
\resumeSubHeadingListStart

\resumeProject
  {SIGNPOST-Bench: Benchmarking Text--Vision Conflict Resolution in MLLMs}
  {First Author \& Project Lead, supervised by Prof. Fan Zhang (PKU) \,|\, [P.1], under review at AAAI 2027}
  {2025 -- Present}
  {{}}
\resumeItemListStart
  \item Proposed a counterfactual benchmark that isolates text--vision conflict in multimodal reasoning: 5,111 image groups and 25,555 variants across four geo-localization datasets, with an automated pipeline for adversarial text and scene-coherent image edits; code and dataset released publicly.
  \item Evaluated 20 MLLMs from seven providers and found that adversarial text increases median prediction error by 4.8$\times$; introduced the \textbf{Multimodal Conflict Robustness Score (MCRS)}, showing that conflict-resolution capability is orthogonal to clean-input accuracy and does not scale with model size.
\resumeItemListEnd

\resumeProject
  {GLARE (Geolocation Leakage Attention \& Reasoning Engine)}
  {Project Lead, co-supervised by Prof. Fan Zhang (PKU) and Prof. Huanfa Chen (UCL)}
  {2025 -- Present}
  {{}}
\resumeItemListStart
  \item Designed an MLLM interpretability framework that triangulates white-box cross-attention grounding, verbalized clue analysis, and human mouse-attention proxies to study geographic privacy leakage in street-view imagery.
  \item Built pipelines for attention extraction, calibrated risk scoring, and bias analysis (bootstrap CI, FDR correction, effect sizes), covering internal--output alignment, model--human attention gaps, predictive validity, and systematic bias.
\resumeItemListEnd

\resumeProject
  {MACRO-City Engine: Stock--Flow Diagnostics of Urban ``Fragile Booms''}
  {First Author, with Prof. Michael Batty and Prof. Huanfa Chen (UCL CASA) \,|\, Manuscript in preparation}
  {2025 -- Present}
  {{}}
\resumeItemListStart
  \item Building an ecology-informed stock--flow diagnostic across 302 cities in 135 countries, integrating 12 satellite and geospatial data streams to separate fast-changing surface and atmospheric processes (Flow) from persistent built and ecological context (Stock); validated using COVID-19 as an exogenous shock.
\resumeItemListEnd

\resumeProject
  {Urban Causal Representation Learning}
  {First Author, supervised by Prof. Yunhan Zheng (PKU) \,|\, Ongoing}
  {Summer 2026 -- Present}
  {{}}
\resumeItemListStart
  \item Developing a causal representation-learning framework that learns transferable, grid-level representations of heterogeneous treatment responses from multi-source urban panel data (housing, POI, population, nighttime lights), using matching, generalized synthetic control, and matrix completion to construct causal response labels for treated grids.
\resumeItemListEnd
